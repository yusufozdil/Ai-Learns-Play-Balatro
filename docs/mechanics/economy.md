# Economy (round loop)

Sources: https://balatrowiki.org/w/Money , https://balatrowiki.org/w/Interest , https://balatrowiki.org/w/Shop , https://balatrowiki.org/w/Vouchers , https://balatrowiki.org/w/Tags , https://balatrowiki.org/w/Blinds_and_Antes , https://balatrowiki.org/w/Gold_Card . Blind rewards, interest base rule, selling are in blinds.md; this file adds the remainder. Shop prices are out of scope.

## Interest cap changes
- Base: $1 per $5 held, max $5 (money over $25 earns nothing) (Interest, Money).
- Seed Money voucher: cap $10 (money $50) (Vouchers, Interest).
- Money Tree voucher (requires Seed Money in the usual voucher chain; unlock: max interest for ten consecutive rounds): cap $20 (money $100) (Vouchers, Interest).
- To the Moon joker: +$1 per $5 held on top of the base $1; both vouchers + To the Moon give max $40 (Interest). Whether the cap applies before or after the To the Moon doubling is implied: $20 cap x2 = $40 (Interest); recommended: compute base interest, cap it, then add the joker's extra equal amount.
- No interest: Green Deck, Omelette, Mad World (Money).

## Reroll (shop)
- Base cost $5, +$1 per reroll in the same shop, resets to the starting price when a new shop is entered (Shop). Reroll Surplus: starting price $3 (-$2 wiki says "rerolls cost $2 less"); Reroll Glut: $1; Chaos the Clown: first reroll each shop free; D6 Tag: next shop starts at $0 (Shop).
- Price progression with Surplus: starting price $3 then +$1 per reroll `UNVERIFIED` but implied by "lowers the starting price".
- Rerolls do not restock booster packs or the voucher (Shop).
- Boss reroll (Director's Cut/Retcon/Boss Tag) is a separate $10 cost, only noted (Blinds_and_Antes).

## Money sources in the round loop
1. During play: Gold Seal on scored card $3 per trigger (scoring.md); Lucky card $20 on 1/15; joker effects on scoring/discard (joker task).
2. End of round: Gold card in hand $3 (Red Seal $6) (Gold_Card), counted in interest base.
3. Cash Out: blind reward (Small $3, Big $4, Boss $5, Showdown $8; Red Stake+ Small $0), $1 per unused hand, joker end-of-round money (Golden Joker, Rocket, Cloud 9, etc.), Investment Tag $25 after boss, interest (Money).
4. Tags on skip: Economy (doubles money, +$40 max), Speed $5 per skipped blind this run, Handy $1 per hand played this run, Garbage $1 per unused discard this run, Investment $25 (Tags).
5. Selling jokers/consumables: floor(cost/2), min $1, plus Egg/Gift Card bonuses (blinds.md section 8).
6. Boss: The Ox sets money to $0 (bosses.md).

## Money sinks
- Shop purchases (jokers, consumables, vouchers, packs, playing cards), rerolls, boss rerolls ($10).
- The Tooth: $1 per card played.
- Rental jokers $3 per round (not White Stake).
- Debt: only Credit Card allows negative money, down to -$20 (The_Ox page https://balatrowiki.org/w/The_Ox).

## Starting money
$4 for Red Deck per notes.md §4.1 (not on fetched wiki pages; `UNVERIFIED`). Yellow Deck starts +$10 (Interest).
